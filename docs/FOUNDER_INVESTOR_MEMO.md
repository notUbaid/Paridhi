## 2. Why Founders Cannot Solve This With a Simple SaaS or Pure Hardware

If this problem could be solved by a simple ChatGPT wrapper, a Next.js landing page, or a camera sold in a retail box, it would have been commoditized long ago. It has remained unsolved because it sits at the intersection of **three difficult domains**:

1. **Physical Ground Reality & Energy Physics**: Running a reliable camera on an empty piece of dirt without electricity or broadband through 3 weeks of monsoonal rain and 45°C summer heat, while making it theft-proof against local vandals.
2. **The Human Verification Layer**: In India, local encroachers and corrupt village clerks do not respect a camera alone. You need verified local field agents who walk the perimeter monthly, check all 4 boundary stones, and interview immediate neighbors.
3. **The State Land Revenue Registry & Legal Action**: Encroachment usually begins at the Tahsildar's office, not at the gate. You must continuously monitor state revenue portals (Telangana Dharani, Karnataka Bhoomi, Punjab Jamabandi) and have partner High Court advocates ready to file Section 148A CPC caveat petitions within 48 hours.
